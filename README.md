## 🚀 Overview

Official portfolio website of **Alesjo Agolli**, engineered with **React 18**, **TypeScript**, **Vite**, and **Tailwind CSS** in an architectural **Obsidian Black (`#060708`) × Electric Cyan (`#00F0FF`)** design system.

### Featured Deployments
- **["Don Bosko Tirana" Digital Ecosystem](https://www.qendradonbosko.com/)** — Official Web Portal, *"Sekretaria"* Administrative Platform & *"Bosko AI"* Assistant (`React`, `TypeScript`, `Node.js`, `Supabase`, `Ollama AI`, `Tailwind CSS`)
- **[Luka Rent a Car](https://lukarentacar.com)** — Commercial Mobile-First Vehicle Rental & Booking Platform (`React`, `Vite`, `Tailwind CSS`, `Node.js`)
- **[HaNeul — Asian Food Distributor Platform](https://haneul.blackbear-solutions.com/)** — Multi-Page Product Discovery & Brand Platform (`Figma`, `React`, `Tailwind CSS`)
